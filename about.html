About Metanous

An independent, member-owned consultancy. We have nothing to sell you except the work.

Why we exist

Most migrations that go wrong were never drawn. Someone moved the servers, then found the shared mailbox, the undocumented workflow and the vendor app that depended on them.

Metanous was founded in Texas in 2026 by four practitioners who had spent their careers on those projects. Our rule is simple: the architecture is documented before anything moves, and you keep the documents.

We are independent. We recommend what fits your estate and your budget, and we train your people to run it.

At a glance

  • Metanous LLC, a Texas limited liability company
  • Based in Irving, Texas
  • Owned and run by its four founding members
  • Practice built on Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365 and Azure OpenAI
  • Working with organizations in the USA, UK, Middle East and Asia

How we work

Four phases, in this order. Each one ends with something written down that you keep.

  1. Discover and assess

    We inventory what you run, who depends on it and what it costs. You get a written assessment.

  2. Design and plan

    We document the target architecture and the migration order before anything moves.

  3. Migrate and modernize

    We move workloads in waves, with a pilot group, rollback points and a cutover runbook.

  4. Operate and optimize

    We hand over with training and documentation, then tune cost, security and performance.
